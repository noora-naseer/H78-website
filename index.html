Good to know

Quick answers.

Are local islands alcohol-free?

Alcohol isn't served on local islands, so we run sunset mocktails and fresh kurumba instead. Licensed resort excursions can be arranged on request.

What should I wear?

Beachwear is great at the designated bikini beach and on boat trips. In the village, light shoulder- and knee-covering clothes are respectful.

How does the boat transfer work?

You land, we meet you at the jetty. Use the planner above for your departure time and live countdown. Prices are fixed and shown before you book.

Is the Wi-Fi really good enough to work?

h78 Veli's rooftop co-working deck runs Starlink at 250+ Mbps in our tests. Video calls are fine, and desks have power at every seat.

Can I cancel?

Flexible cancellation is available on direct bookings. Message us on WhatsApp for the policy that applies to your dates.
